What is Automatic Writing

Divine wisdom flowing through you

Have you ever wondered how to tap into the wisdom of the universe to receive guidance that could change your life? Automatic writing is a simple yet powerful process that will help you connect with your higher self, your spirit guides, Source, and your creativity, so you can get the answers and ideas you've been seeking.

It's perfect when you need clarity on a situation or help making a decision, whether about work, relationships, or personal challenges. Connecting to divine wisdom can help you find ideal solutions and get the ideas flowing.

A note on consistency

Consistency is key in any spiritual practice. Create a routine around your automatic writing sessions so your mind and body know it's time to connect. The more you practice, the stronger the connection will become. This sacred practice will eventually become second nature.

Step One

Safety Checklist

01
Set Clear Intentions

Your thoughts carry great power. Before beginning, state your intentions out loud to create a protective barrier for your session.

02
Call in Protective Beings

Invite Archangel Michael or another protective being into your space to shield you from any lower energies during your session.

03
Pillar of Light Visualization

Draw energy up from the Earth through your body and into God's light. Then visualize divine energy flowing back down, forming a shield of light around you.

Sample Intention Invocation

"I intend to connect with only beings of love and light that serve God's plan and are of the highest energy and frequency."

Calling In Archangel Michael

"Archangel Michael, I call upon you to guard me during this automatic writing session. Please keep any beings that are not of the light away from me as I open myself up during this sacred practice."

Step Two

Creating Your Sacred Space

Your sacred space is your sanctuary, a place where you feel completely safe and protected. It doesn't have to be an entire room. A cozy corner of your bedroom, a nook, or even a mental space you create in your mind can serve. The key is to make this space feel high-vibrational and comforting.

01
Choose Your Space

Pick a spot where you feel peaceful and undisturbed. Consistency matters, so try to use the same spot every time.

02
Energise Your Space

Fill it with things that raise your vibration: crystals, candles, incense, oracle cards, images of divine beings, soft blankets, and cushions.

03
Clear the Space

Light incense, sage, or use a cleansing spray. As the smoke fills the room, visualize it clearing away any lingering energy, leaving only pure light.

Step Three

Prepare Your Energy

01
Ground

Imagine roots going down from your feet into the Earth. Visualize any negative energy flowing through those roots, leaving your body and being absorbed by the Earth.

02
Pillar of Light

Do the pillar of light visualization where you visualize energy from Mother Earth and God flowing through you. Imagine you are back in God's white light.

03
Raise Your Vibe

If you're feeling low, take a few minutes to dance, listen to music, or breathe deeply until you feel uplifted and in a high-vibrational state.

Step Four

Clear Your Mind Clutter

If you're worried about distractions, take a few minutes before you start writing to jot down anything that's on your mind. By acknowledging these thoughts, you set them aside so they don't interfere with your session.

Step Five

Set Your Intention

01
Pick a Guide

Decide who you want to connect with, whether it's your higher self, a specific guide, or God. State this clearly before you begin.

02
Clarify Your Needs

Let your guides know what guidance you're seeking, what issue you're working through, or what decision you want to make. Ask for the best guide to step forward.

03
Trust They Are With You

Imagine a room filled with your spiritual team: your guides, angels, and higher self, nodding and understanding exactly what you've asked for. See one stepping forward, ready to assist.

Step Six

Connect & Receive

01
Write Down Your Prompt

Write a question at the top of your journal page to set the intention and focus your session. Use the prompts in the next section to get started.

02
Trust You Are Receiving

Close your eyes and visualize your crown chakra opening like a flower. Picture an antenna extending from your crown, reaching up to the divine and drawing down light and information.

03
Just Start Writing

Don't worry about spelling or grammar. Just let whatever comes through flow onto the page. Even if it feels like you're making it up, keep going. The more you write, the more you'll recognise the voice of your guides.

Prompts

To Get You Started

Use a prompt at the top of your journal page to help set the intention for your session and to help you focus on receiving the guidance you seek.

What messages do my guides have for me today?
What do I need to know that is for my highest good today?
What is the next step in my career? What do I need to know about my career?
What is the next step on my spiritual journey?
How can I best overcome the current challenge that I'm facing with respect to _______ in a way that is for my highest and best good?

A gentle reminder

Spirit cannot tell you what to do. Guidance comes through as insights, clarity, and perspective. The choices always remain yours.
